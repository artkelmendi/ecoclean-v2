# EcoClean visual direction

Industrial precision with the care of hospitality. Retain the blue identity, Poppins display type, Mulish body type, ink and pale-blue surfaces, and generous space.

Sections play timed entrances once when they enter view, then stay settled. Scroll position does not scrub or reverse animation. Photography opens through shaped masks, copy arrives in short coordinated sequences, and the Streamline indicator advances in discrete timed steps. Reduced motion restores static presentation.

Catalog opening uses CSS from the first paint to avoid hydration flashes. Page previews animate when their images finish loading. Supported browsers crossfade document navigation; other browsers retain normal navigation with destination entrances. The hero is prepared before the intro opens and reveals on both first and repeat visits.

The intro leads with the original symbol, then reveals the outlined Eco Clean wordmark and a small blue seam. Two soft panels open from the center between 1.15 and 1.85 seconds, while the hero entrance begins beneath them. A pre-paint preference check prevents a white overlay flash on returning visits, deep links and reduced-motion visits. Skip and Escape dismiss immediately; scroll locking and background inertness are restored on completion. The hero film alternates forward and reverse using encoded frames, with explicit playback controls.

Browser details use the same blue identity: a rounded blue scrollbar on a pale track and small native SVG arrow/hand cursors on fine hover-capable pointers. There is no custom scrolling engine or pointer-tracking loop. Text inputs and disabled controls retain their appropriate cursors, touch devices use their normal behavior, and forced-color mode restores system cursor and scrollbar styling.

The dedicated catalog uses a quiet editorial layout, lightweight page previews, chapter navigation, page controls and selectable source text. The complete original PDF remains available on demand.
